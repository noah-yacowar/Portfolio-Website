6-DOF Auto-Calibrator - Force-torque sensor calibration

Software for a first-of-its-kind auto-calibrator for 6-DOF force-torque sensors, cutting calibration runs from 2 hours to 15 minutes.

  • Incremental P controller with load-cell force feedback, driving voice coil actuators to precise target loads
  • Motor calibration routine mapping current to force output, minimizing overshoot and force-moment crosstalk

04

AI-MU - Wearable mocap

Modular, wearable real-time motion capture built from custom IMU trackers, with live visualization in Unity.

  • Sensor calibration that accounts for body offset errors and custom mounting positions
  • Live skeleton built with forward kinematics, extending outward from the hip
  • Custom tracker PCB: ESP8266 + BNO080 IMU streaming over Wi-Fi at 100 Hz
  • Two-part SolidWorks enclosure for each tracker, plus a latching wearable holder it clips into
  • Export tracking data to BVH or FBX for animation pipelines
